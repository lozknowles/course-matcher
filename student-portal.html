STEP 3 · DfE EDUCATION RECORD

Request the learner’s verified education record

The live DfE service can be integrated through an MIS vendor API, or staff can use the DfE service to request, view and download learner records.

DfE-supported journey
  1. 1
    Lincoln sends a record request

    Through an integrated MIS or the DfE View Education Records service.

  2. 2
    Learner receives it in the app

    The learner reviews the request and decides whether to share.

  3. 3
    Record becomes available

    Name, DOB, address, schools, official qualifications and support information can be included.

  4. 4
    Lincoln verifies and continues enrolment

    No OCR is needed when the shared record supplies the evidence.
